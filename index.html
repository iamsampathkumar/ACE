About the College

Tirupur's First Professional Degree Institution, Since 2007

Angel College of Engineering and Technology is sponsored by Angel Institutions, a public charitable trust founded by industrialist Engineer D. Sachithanantham, B.E., President of the Angel Group of Companies. Approved by AICTE, New Delhi and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai, ACET was the first professional degree-level institution to open in Tirupur — a town known for its enterprising, industrial spirit.

The college's flourishing industrial surroundings feed directly into student employability, with state-of-the-art laboratories upgraded every year, an LCD projector in every department and a central library holding more than 20,000 volumes. The ISO 9001:2015-certified, 18.66-acre green campus is built to meet the demands of a fast-moving corporate sector.

Guiding Principles

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a center of academic excellence by imparting quality technical education through continuous improvement.

To instill in students the drive and interest to be competitive at a global level.

To grow into a Deemed University.

Our Mission “

Angel College of Engineering and Technology is devoted to equipping students with the knowledge, skill and attitude to continuously improve the standard of living of individuals, families, industries and society, on par with global standards, through quality education in Engineering and Technology. ACET nurtures every student to dream and realise professional growth in a congenial environment shaped by a versatile faculty, instilling the values and character needed for socio-economic and technological growth at a global level.
